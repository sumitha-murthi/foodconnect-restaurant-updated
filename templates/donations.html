Charity Donation Lifecycles

  • Publication Time Gate: Charities can only see and claim donations once the publication date and time arrive.
  • Strict Collection Deadlines: Once the deadline passes, any unclaimed food is automatically marked as EXPIRED.
  • Inventory Deduction: Donated meals are immediately deducted from kitchen inventory to prevent double-selling.
  • Partial Quantity Claims: Charities can claim partial portions (e.g., 5 out of 15), leaving the rest open for other NGOs!
